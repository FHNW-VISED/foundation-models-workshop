Why the pets mask was wrong

The green in your screenshot was the ground truth, not a prediction. Oxford's trimaps have three classes: pet, background, and a "not sure" band drawn 10–20 px wide around every outline. Our data script counted that band as pet, so the mask spilled onto the rug and the sofa. Switch between the two versions:

38,440 px

pet pixels now. The 18,368 px band is drawn grey and left out of IoU and out of the heads' loss.

On this image the band was 32 % of the mask. Across the 60 held-out pets it covers 10.7 % of the image on average (3 % to 20 %). The pets heads score higher now. The five-pet head went from 0.73 to 0.88 IoU because it is no longer penalised on pixels nobody can judge. The notebook uses the band as a small example of the whole workshop: even the dataset's own annotators say "unknown" instead of "background".

Why the ladder cell was slow

SAM itself was not the slow part. clDice needs the centre line of every mask, and thinning a mask that covers the whole fundus takes about 0.8 s. Many of SAM's retina masks cover 90–100 %. Those masks now get their centre line at half resolution, which gave the same clDice on our crops, and a cache stops the same mask being thinned twice.

SAMclDice centre lines

Seconds for three real 1024 px retina crops, four prompts each, on the Mac CPU. On a T4 the SAM part shrinks, so before the fix the centre lines were nearly all of the wait.
