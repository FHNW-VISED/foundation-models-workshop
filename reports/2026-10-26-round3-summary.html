The 16 px patch is the main limit. With five complete masks, a U-Net that sees the pixels doubles the notebook's head. It needs those complete masks, though: on strokes alone it paints the whole fundus and falls below the head on DINOv3 features. That is the honest case for the workshop's methods, and the new 10.3 shows it next to your session's bars. Choosing the cut on the training images is a free +0.05 for every head; I have not put that into the learners' path, because that is your call.

Sources: Little W-Net (arXiv:2009.01907, Table 2, Dice 0.810 on HRF) and ESRVS (arXiv:2607.24453, Table 3, Dice 0.726 with one labelled image). Both papers use other splits and image sizes, so treat them as orders of magnitude. Details are in reports/2026-10-26-tuning/ and reports/2026-10-26-hrf-state-of-the-art.md.
