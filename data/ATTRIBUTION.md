# Data and model attribution

Everything the workshop ships or downloads, with its source and licence. The specialised dataset is
whatever `data/specialised.json` points to (provisional pick: HRF retina, see `reports/bakeoff.md`).
`data/smoke/` holds 8 reduced images (4 per dataset) committed for the headless test; the workshop
subsets live in `data/cache/workshop/` and are hosted separately for participants.

## Derived files

The masks are the datasets' own ground truth, resized or cropped. The incomplete supervision
(`scribbles/`, `visible/`), the SAM pseudo-labels, the DINOv3 features and the head checkpoints are
generated by `scripts/prepare_data.py` and `scripts/precompute.py` from that ground truth and carry the
same licence as their source images (Pet derivatives: CC BY-SA 4.0; HRF derivatives: CC BY 4.0).
